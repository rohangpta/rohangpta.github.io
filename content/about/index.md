I enjoy thinking about systems, programming languages, complexity, and game theory. Here's some of my favorites:

- [No Silver Bullet](https://www.cs.unc.edu/techreports/86-020.pdf) — "Automatic programming is simply a euphemism for a higher level language." Fred Brooks, 1986
- [Hyrum's Law](https://www.hyrumslaw.com/)
- [The Zen of Python](https://peps.python.org/pep-0020/) — `>>> import this`
- [Conway's Law](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/ConwaysLaw.html)
- [Gall's Law](https://www.laws-of-software.com/laws/gall/) — "A complex system that works has evolved from a simple system that worked."
- [Maxwell's Demon: Computation is Energy](https://thundersaidenergy.com/2024/04/04/maxwells-demon-computation-is-energy/)
- [Links between Entropy, Complexity, and the Technological Singularity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.10844) — Theodore Modis, 2024
- [The Rise of "Worse is Better"](https://www.jwz.org/doc/worse-is-better.html) — "The virus-like spread of simplicity." Richard Gabriel, 1989
- [As We May Think](https://worrydream.com/refs/Bush_1945_-_As_We_May_Think_(Life_Magazine).pdf) — Vannevar Bush, 1945
- [The Lindy Effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindy_effect)
